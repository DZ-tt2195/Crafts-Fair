Rules	Rules
Rules_Summary	Each Turn: \n1. Choose a token type and remove a counter from that token's Event. \n2. Add 1 of that token at level 1, then upgrade 1 token of that type. \n3. You may sell 2+ tokens and 2+ cards all at once to score CoinIcon. \n\nBetween Turns:\n4. For each Event with 0 counters, each player draws 2 cards and follows its instructions. \n5. Add the counters back onto those Events (2 per player). \n6. The first player to reach 20+ CoinIcon wins.
