Rules	Rules
Rules_Summary	Each Turn: \n1. Either add a token at rank 1, or advance 1 token on your grid. \n2. Remove a counter from that token's Twist. \n3. Submit 2+ tokens and 2+ placards at once to score crowns. \n\nBetween Turns:\n4. For each Twist with 0 counters, each player draws 2 placards and follows its instructions. \n5. Add the counters back onto those Twists (2 per player). \n6. The first player to reach 20+ crowns wins.
