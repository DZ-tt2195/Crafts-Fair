Rules	Rules
Rules_Summary	Each Turn: \n1. Choose a token type and remove a counter from that token's Twist. \n2. Add 1 of that token with a value of 1, then advance 1 token of that type. \n3. You may submit 2+ tokens and 2+ placards at once to score crowns. \n\nBetween Turns:\n4. For each Twist with 0 counters, each player draws 2 placards and follows its instructions. \n5. Add the counters back onto those Twists (2 per player). \n6. The first player to reach 20+ crowns wins.
