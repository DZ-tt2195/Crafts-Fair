Rules	Rules
Rules_Summary	Each Turn: \n1. Choose any TokenIcon (token) and remove a counter from that token's Strategy. \n2. Create 1 of that token at 1 LevelIcon, then upgrade 1 token of that type to the next LevelIcon. \n3. You may sell 2+ TokenIcon and 2+ CardIcon, all at once, to score CoinIcon. \n\nBetween Turns:\n4. For each Strategy with 0 counters, each player draws 2 CardIcon and follows its instructions. \n5. Add the counters back onto those Strategies (2 per player). \n6. The first player to reach 20+ CoinIcon wins.
